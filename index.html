code

I train off data, so I build the data fields I couldn't buy: extensions for the Hammerhead Karoo bike computer, written in Kotlin on Hammerhead's karoo-ext SDK. Free and open source.

  • MTB Dynamics — test builds
    Trail metrics for the Karoo: Grit, Flow, jumps, cornering and descents, joined with Flight Attendant, AXS and power data.
  • Workout+ — 1.0, Karoo 2
    The Karoo 3's workout layout on the Karoo 2: target bar, big interval countdown, interval graph.
  • CORE Heat — needs a CORE sensor
    Heat training fields: core and skin temperature, Heat Strain Index, heat zone, training load and adaptation.

Older: my macOS dotfiles, a site for Kalograia — the village my family is from, on the wrong side of the line since 1974 — and the operating systems coursework I still keep around. More on all of them here.
