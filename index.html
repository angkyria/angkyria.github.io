work

Lasercharge LTD — Nicosia, since Jan 2020. Team leader since 2025.

I own the first-line queue for our enterprise client base across banking, pharmaceuticals, financial services and market research — taking the calls, triaging and diagnosing at first contact, closing what can be closed remotely, and putting the rest in front of the right engineer. Alongside that I plan the daily workload for a team of 9–15 field and desk technicians, run the spare-parts warehouse and all procurement so no support commitment ever waits on availability, and handle HP warranty claims from diagnosis through to closure.

On the systems side I administer customer Microsoft 365 tenants — identity, licensing, Exchange Online, security and access policies — plus WatchGuard Firebox firewalls and Panda endpoint protection across the client base. When a fault is genuinely hard, it escalates to me.

Before all of that, sixteen years at E.X. Podilates, the family bicycle import and repair business — the workshop bench first, then imports, distribution, international trade expos, and the Dolibarr ERP the whole operation ran on.

racing

I race cross-country mountain bike for Cyprus — XCO, XCC and XCE — with a second place at the 2026 national championships at Troodos, and I'm currently leading the 2026 Cyprus MTB Cup.

I train on the island's roughest ground, off data rather than feel, around thirteen hours a week. Running fills the other half of the year, kept easy on purpose apart from one target race each winter.
