Repositories

A few things I keep on GitHub.

karoo extensions

Extensions for the Hammerhead Karoo bike computer, written in Kotlin on Hammerhead's karoo-ext SDK. Free and open source under Apache-2.0; each one installs from the APK on its releases page.

MTB Dynamics — test builds, Karoo 2 & 3

Trail metrics worked out from the Karoo's accelerometer and gyroscope: Grit, Flow, jumps and airtime, cornering, descents and an MTB score, with the ride split into climbs and descents. With RockShox Flight Attendant, SRAM AXS and a power meter paired it adds a suspension coach, gear and power use per terrain, and a service tracker. The summary goes to your phone over ntfy and into the ride's FIT file, so intervals.icu and Garmin Connect can read it.

website and demo ride report · source

Workout+ — 1.0, Karoo 2

The Karoo 3's workout layout on the Karoo 2: a target bar that shows in or out of range at a glance, ramps held to their current value, a big interval countdown, an interval graph colored by your zones, and up to four data fields of your choice. With a CORE sensor it adds a heat row, using CORE Heat's figures when that's installed.

website · source

CORE Heat — needs a CORE sensor

Heat training data fields driven by a CORE body temperature sensor: core and skin temperature, the Heat Strain Index read straight from the sensor, CORE's heat zones, today's heat training load and a heat adaptation score, in CORE's own zone colors. The heat data goes into the ride's FIT file as well.
